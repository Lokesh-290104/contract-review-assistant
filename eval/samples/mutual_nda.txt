MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 5 March 2026 between Orion Lighting Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Orion"), and Kumar Hardware Stores, a partnership registered in Pune ("Kumar"). Orion and Kumar each a "Party".

1. Purpose
The Parties wish to discuss a possible distribution partnership for LED lighting products (the "Purpose") and will share confidential information for that Purpose only.

2. Confidential Information
Each Party shall hold the other Party's confidential information in strict confidence, use it only for the Purpose, and not disclose it to anyone except its employees who need to know it for the Purpose.

3. Exclusions
Information is not confidential if it is already public, was known to the receiving Party before disclosure, or is independently developed without use of the other Party's information.

4. Term
This Agreement takes effect on the date above and remains in force for two (2) years.

5. Ending the Agreement
Either Party may end this Agreement at any time by giving fourteen (14) days' written notice to the other Party. Confidentiality duties for information already shared continue for two (2) years after the Agreement ends.

6. Return of Information
On request, each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's confidential information.

7. Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, USA.
