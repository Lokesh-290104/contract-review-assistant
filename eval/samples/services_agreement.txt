MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between Brightline Software Private Limited, a company incorporated in India with its office at 14 MG Road, Bengaluru ("Provider"), and Sharma Electricals LLP, with its office at 22 Station Road, Jaipur ("Client"). Provider and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Services" means the inventory software, hosting and support services described in Schedule A.
1.2 "Confidential Information" means any non-public business, technical or financial information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether in writing or orally.

2. TERM
2.1 This Agreement commences on 1 January 2026 (the "Effective Date") and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months.
2.2 At the end of the initial term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 Client shall pay Provider a monthly subscription fee of INR 45,000 plus applicable taxes.
3.2 Provider will invoice Client monthly in advance. Client shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.
3.3 Late payments will carry interest at 1.5% per month until paid.

4. TERMINATION
4.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other Party ninety (90) days' written notice.
4.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure it within thirty (30) days of being notified.
4.3 On termination, Client shall pay all fees due up to the date of termination.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
5.1 Each Party shall keep the other Party's Confidential Information secret and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.
5.2 The obligations in this Section 5 survive for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
6.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental or consequential damages, including loss of profits.
6.2 Each Party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.
7.2 The courts at Bengaluru, Karnataka shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from this Agreement.

8. GENERAL
8.1 Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above.
8.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties and replaces all earlier discussions.
